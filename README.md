## 🚀 What Is heyclaw?

Heyclaw is a free voice assistant that listens to you, understands what you say, and talks back—all on your own Windows computer. It uses advanced AI to handle your requests in real time, without any monthly fees or shady cloud setups. You speak, and heyclaw responds instantly with natural-sounding voice.

Think of it like having a personal assistant that you can talk to anytime, and everything runs right on your machine. No sending your conversations to remote servers for processing.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Real-Time Voice Conversations** – Speak and hear responses immediately with low latency.
- **Wake-Word Activation** – Just say the phrase you pick to get its attention. No buttons needed.
- **Smart AI Brain** – Powered by Google Gemini AI, it understands complex requests and context.
- **Memory** – It remembers what you talked about before, so you can pick up where you left off.
- **Tool Integration** – Connect it to your own apps and services using Model Context Protocol (MCP).
- **Natural Voice Output** – ElevenLabs technology gives it a humanlike, expressive voice.
- **Customizable** – Change the wake word, voice, and more without coding.

## 🎤 How to Use It

1. Launch heyclaw from your Start menu or desktop icon. A small status window will open on your screen.
2. Wait 5–10 seconds for the assistant to start listening.
3. Say your wake word (default: “Hey Claw”) followed by your question or command.
4. Start talking! Speak clearly, and heyclaw will answer you in a natural voice.

**Example commands:**
- “Hey Claw, what’s the weather today?”
- “Hey Claw, remind me to buy milk tomorrow.”
- “Hey Claw, what can you do?”

## 🔧 Configuration (Custom Setup)

You can change how heyclaw behaves:

- **Wake Word:** Choose a phrase that turns on the assistant (e.g., “Computer,” “Jarvis”).
- **Voice:** Pick from several voices from ElevenLabs (male, female, different accents).
- **Memory:** Choose how long the assistant remembers your conversations (erase daily, weekly, or never forget).
- **Tools:** Enable MCP tools to connect heyclaw to your own apps (like calendars, email, or smart home devices). This offers optional for advanced users.

To access settings, click the gear icon in the upper-right corner of the heyclaw window.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

Heyclaw is designed with privacy normally:

- Runs entirely on your PC. No data sent to outside servers except for voice processing (which you turn off in settings).
- No account required to start using it.
- All conversations are stored locally unless you configure external tools.
